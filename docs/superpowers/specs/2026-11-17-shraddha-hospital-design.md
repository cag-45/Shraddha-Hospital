## 1. Intent & Redesign Rationale
Following review, the dark-themed quaternary aesthetic of Template 1 was replaced with **Template 02: "The Compassionate Haven"**.
The dark theme was perceived as sterile, heavy, and overly technical. Template 2 transforms the site into a warm, serene, human-centered healing sanctuary:
- **Visual Warmth:** Italian Alabaster canvas (`#FBF9F5`), Deep Forest Spruce typography (`#142820`), soft Terracotta accents (`#D97757`), and Pale Sage accents (`#E8EFE9`).
- **Eliminates Medical Anxiety:** Focuses on human empathy, peaceful private recovery suites, daylight infusion, and the gentle, attentive bedside manner of Dr. B. Raghuram Reddy.
- **3D Spatial Walkthrough:** Interactive Three.js spatial view of a sunlit private recovery suite and zen courtyard garden with 360° mouse-driven orbit.
- **24-Hour Healing Journey:** Scrollytelling narrative detailing bedside check-in, painless sub-millimeter diode laser intervention, and same-day recovery with 100% cashless TPA discharge.
- **Verified Hospital Data:** Complete address in Yamnampet, Rampally (501302), phone `+91 99895 13630`, domain `shraddhahospitals.co.in`, 4.9★ rating with 100+ verified Google and Justdial reviews, and interactive light-cartography map in the footer.
